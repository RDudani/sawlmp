South Asian Women in the Law · Mentorship Program

Shifting the Narrative for Racialized Women in Law

"Through sharing your experiences both positive and negative, you are not only reclaiming your own voice and seat at the table, but also supporting other women by pulling out a chair for them."

The objective of this program is to provide targeted, concrete mentorship opportunities that account for two crucial identity factors: gender and ethnicity. Studies demonstrate that gender and ethnicity create unique power dynamics, challenges, and opportunities that racialized licensees encounter upon entering the legal profession.

Our Mission & Safe Space

Founded in Canada by lawyer Naiyna Sharma to provide direct, identity-conscious mentorship across courts, firms, and legal organizations.

A Safe, Tailored Space

This program pairs racialized licensees with Mentors in the legal profession who share similar identity factors in order to afford access to a richer mentorship relationship.

  • Discuss barriers encountered in career development openly and confidentially
  • Acquire real strategies to overcome obstacles and advance in your legal practice
  • Foster confidence and step fully into your professional power

The Fundamentals of Mentoring

  • Voluntary: Driven by passion and peer community support
  • Confidential: A private, honest space to address challenges
  • Reciprocal Learning: An opportunity to hone leadership, coaching, and perspective
  • Mutually Beneficial: Enhancing effectiveness across all stages of the legal journey

How Placements Work

Placements are made on a rolling basis. Pairs are matched by practice area, common interests, and tailored mentee goals.

For Mentors

Mentors coach, provide constructive feedback, and share lived experiences navigating firms, courts, and companies as racialized women lawyers.

  • Encourage mentees to build confidence and navigate challenges
  • Provide timely counsel and career guidance
  • Support fellow women while expanding your own leadership perspective

For Mentees

Mentees initiate contact once matched. Pairs receive guiding questions and an optional sample mentoring agreement.

  • Receive email notification with contact info & shared background highlights
  • Check-ins conducted at the half-way mark and closing feedback point
  • Open to law students, licensing candidates, and practicing lawyers/paralegals
